Work comes to you

Kazi inakufuata

No waiting at the gate for work, and no chasing payment.

  1. Go online

    Once your ID is checked, tap Go online when you're ready. Job requests near you pop up with the pay, and you have 30 seconds to accept.

  2. Go to the client

    Get directions in any map app. The family sees you coming on their map.

  3. Do the job

    Tap Start work and Finish job. The family pays by M-Pesa or cash, and rates your work.

  4. Cash out

    M-Pesa payments land in your wallet. Withdraw to your M-Pesa the same day.

You keep 80%, plus transport and every tip

Jua malipo kabla ya kukubali

  • See the pay first: every job offer shows exactly what you earn before you accept.
  • Transport is yours: your matatu or boda fare is added to the price and paid to you.
  • Tips are 100% yours: families can add a tip when they pay.
  • Busy days pay more: weekends, public holidays and the festive season cost families more, and your 80% grows with it.

What you need to join

Unachohitaji

  • A smartphone: Android 8 or newer, or an iPhone with iOS 17 or newer.
  • A Safaricom line in your own name for M-Pesa payouts. Sign up with that number in the app.
  • Your national ID. In the app, tap Get verified and send photos of both sides and a selfie. Our team checks them before your first job, then deletes the photos.
  • A next of kin we can call in an emergency.
  • A DCI Certificate of Good Conduct (optional) for the good-conduct badge. Wasaidizi with it are offered jobs first.

Questions

Why do you need my ID and a selfie?

Families let you into their homes, so we check that every msaidizi is who they say they are. Our team compares your selfie with your ID, then deletes the photos. We keep your details and the result, and you're told as soon as you're approved. If something needs fixing, the app tells you what.

Do I have to accept every job?

No. Decline it, or let the 30 seconds run out, and the job goes to the next msaidizi. Go offline whenever you want to stop.

Who pays for my transport?

The family. Transport is part of the price they see, and it's added to what you earn: KSh 100 within 3 km, then KSh 25 a km.

When do I get my money?

M-Pesa payments reach your wallet as soon as the family pays. Withdraw to your M-Pesa any time you have KSh 100 or more.

What if the family pays cash?

Collect the full amount in cash and tap Cash received. Msaidizi's 20% share is then taken from your next M-Pesa earnings, or you can pay it by M-Pesa.

Is my location shared all the time?

No. Your location is shared only while you're online, so we can send you nearby jobs and the family can see you coming.

When can I download Mfanyikazi?

Mfanyikazi is coming soon to Android and iPhone. We'll put the download links on this page on launch day.
