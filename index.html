Know the price before anyone comes over

Bei wazi. Hakuna kupiga bei.

  • One price for everything: the work, her matatu or boda fare, and a KSh 50 booking fee.
  • Priced for your estate: 61 Kenyan estates and towns are built in.
  • Save with bundles and plans: 10–15% off bundles, up to 18% off weekly or daily help.
  • Busy days cost a little more: weekends, public holidays and 20 Dec – 5 Jan.
  • Transport: KSh 100 within 3 km, then KSh 25 a km, never more than KSh 600.

About Mfanyikazi

Questions

Where does Msaidizi work?

The app knows 61 estates and towns, from Karen, Kilimani and Westlands to Kitengela, Ruiru and Rongai, plus Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and other major towns. We'll open area by area.

How do I pay?

With M-Pesa: when the work is done, a payment prompt comes to your phone and you enter your PIN. You can also pay cash. Tips go 100% to your msaidizi.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancelling is free while we look for a msaidizi, and for 5 minutes after one accepts. After that the fee covers her fare: KSh 150 or the transport cost, whichever is more. Once she has arrived, it's the transport cost plus KSh 150.

Do I need my own cleaning products?

You can use your own, or ask your msaidizi to bring supplies for KSh 300. If you have a washing machine, laundry is 20% cheaper.

Can I book help every week?

Yes. Weekly and daily plans cost up to 18% less than one-off visits.

Who are the wasaidizi?

Independent mama fuas, cleaners and cooks who get jobs through the Mfanyikazi app. Our team checks each one's national ID and a selfie before their first job, and asks for a DCI Certificate of Good Conduct. Every visit is rated.

When can I download it?

Msaidizi is coming soon to Android and iPhone. We'll put the download links on this page on launch day.
